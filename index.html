About Khan Travels

Khan Travels is a demo bus reservation experience built around one idea: a journey should feel easy from the first search to the final stop.

We wanted a booking flow that is calm, readable and honest — clear times, clear fares, and a seat map you can actually understand at a glance. No hidden steps, no surprise charges.

Comfort first

Spacious coaches, sensible departure times and route details shown before you commit.

Transparent pricing

Fares per seat are listed up front. Totals are always the sum of the seats you picked.

Built as a demo

This site runs entirely in your browser with sample routes and demo reservations.
